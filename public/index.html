How it works

Your recommendation is built from the movie itself, not from a popularity list.

01Choose a film

Search the 4,803-title library and pick a movie you already like.

02Read its DNA

We compare genres, keywords, cast, director and story description.

03Find the closest fit

Cosine similarity ranks the ten movies with the most overlap.
